WHERE AISLES & TABLES CONVERGE Nobody remembers the dinner. Everybody remembers the final.

Every security company in this market is buying the same fifteen minutes — a booth, a webinar, a dinner nobody can place a week later. A league is the opposite bargain. You are not on the guest list; you are on the team sheet, for six hours, with people who came to play.

THE AFTERNOONSix sides. One ground.A very long lunch break.

THE CRICKET

Ten overs a side

Tennis ball, turf, two pitches running at once so nobody stands around. Sixty minutes a match, three matches a side — and a final that everyone takes far too seriously.

THE COMPANY

Fifty-four leaders

Security heads from across Bengaluru, drafted into six squads at a live auction. Nine of them spend the day in your colours, on your team sheet, under your captain.

THE EVENING

Then the floodlights

Cocktails, dinner and the awards. The easiest conversation you will have all quarter, because you spent the afternoon on the same side as the person you are having it with.

THE RHYTHM
First ball at two. Trophy by seven. Dinner for as long as it takes.

The Six Six sides. Six owners. No more.

Each side is owned outright by one security company and carries its name all season — on the jersey, on the team zone, on the scoreboard. Every one of the six is still open.

You do not pick in front of the room.

Fifty-four names go up one at a time and the six Owners bid for them live. You are not handed a list of attendees; you build a squad in public, and every leader in the room watches you do it.

By the end of it nine security leaders are wearing your name, and they know exactly who bought them.

What you ownA franchise is not a logoA team.

A sponsorship ends when the day does. A franchise starts at the auction, where you bid in front of the room for the leaders who will wear your name, and it carries through the cricket, the evening, the awards photograph and everything published afterwards.

The ground

Your flag flown at the venue, your banners at the boundary and net side, and your name on the welcome wall as people arrive.

Your team zone

A branded bay of your own beside the pitch — where your squad sits between matches, where you host your own guests, and where the refreshments are all afternoon.

The team

Jersey front, back and cap. Your brand in the team name. A name board on the team zone, and a captain who will take the final far too seriously.

Three of your own

Nine security leaders come to you at the auction. Three places in the squad of twelve are yours to fill with your own people — playing, in the same jersey, not standing at the boundary with a lanyard.

The record

The live scoreboard, the team sheets and score cards, the awards backdrop every photograph is taken against, and every highlight clip that follows the day.

One to a category No competitor in the next dugout.

Your franchise holds your category for the season. No second logo in your space, no shared billing, and no chance of your closest rival owning the side beside yours. Categories are allotted in order of confirmation — ask us what is still open.
