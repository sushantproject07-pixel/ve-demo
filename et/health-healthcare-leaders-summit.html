    India’s healthcare system is expanding rapidly, yet it faces a paradox where fundamentals remain uneven. Hospitals are multiplying across cities, but access, affordability, and workforce distribution continue to pose major challenges. India may have roughly one doctor per 800–900 people (NMC estimates), but most are concentrated in urban centres, leaving rural regions underserved. Simultaneously, medical education costs between Rs 1 crore and Rs 1.5 crore for management and NRI quota MBBS seats in private colleges, reshaping the profession’s economics.

    Meanwhile, private equity and global capital are pouring into the hospital sector, driving consolidation, expansion, and professional management. While investment strengthens infrastructure and scale, it also intensifies debates around pricing, patient trust, and the commercialisation of care.
    A hidden challenge is silent revenue leakage, with 10 to 20 percent of revenue lost because patients do not return for follow-ups. Growth in healthcare is not about more patients or higher investment; it is about building trust, continuity of care, and long-term patient relationships.

    What is ET Healthcare Leaders Summit - A Healthcare Conference for?

    The 6th Edition of the Healthcare Leaders Summit (HLS) hosted by The Economic Times, an annual gathering where policymakers, academicians, and healthcare stakeholders come together to discuss important healthcare issues in India and beyond.

    What is the objective of the Healthcare Leaders Event & conference?

    ET Healthcare Leaders event 2026 will bring the best-known healthcare individuals under one roof. The health conference is going to be both an intense and informative event for key policymakers, groundbreaking industry experts, medical professionals, and patients' representatives as they will share their insights and bring complementary perspectives on key healthcare challenges.
