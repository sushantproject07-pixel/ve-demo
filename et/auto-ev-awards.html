    Overview

    Electric mobility reflects ambition, innovation, and sustainability. The EV Awards honor the pioneers driving India’s transition to cleaner transportation—covering EV manufacturers, battery innovators, charging infrastructure providers, and fleet operators. These awards celebrate technologies and models that make electric mobility accessible, scalable, and impactful.

    Entries will be judged on parameters such as technology innovation, deployment success, adoption scale, and sustainability outcomes. From battery advancements to fast-charging networks, only organizations creating significant breakthroughs will be recognized. Competition will be fierce, as the awards seek to highlight true leaders shaping the future of India’s EV ecosystem.

    How our Awards Work

    Judging Criteria

    We focus on exceptional entries that demonstrate outstanding execution and strong impact.

    Judges consider aspects like innovation, inspiration, and the ability to push boundaries.

    Persistent Fairness & Integrity

    Judges who enter the competition don't vote on entries in their category to avoid bias. A strict code of conduct ensures impartial evaluation of all entries. This process ensures that only the most exceptional and impactful work receives recognition.

    Confidentiality

    Judges do not vote on entries in their own category to avoid bias. A strict code of conduct ensures impartial evaluation of all entries. This process ensures that only the most exceptional and impactful work receives recognition.

    Scrutiny by Expert Tabulator

    • Eligibility Check: An expert tabulator meticulously verifies the eligibility of each entry.
    • Certified Assurance: The tabulator’s name and logo stand as a mark of credibility and transparency.

    Thorough Review Process

    • Expert Judges: We enlist top professionals from across the nation, ensuring judges bring deep industry knowledge and current insights.
    • Two-Tier Judging: A rigorous two-stage judging process narrows down the entries to the best of the best.
    • Final Shortlist: The top 5 entries in each category advance to the final round, highlighting excellence.

    Grand Jury

    • Winner Selection: From the final shortlist, the grand jury identifies the ultimate winners.
    • Ceremony Recognition: Winners are unveiled and celebrated at our prestigious award ceremony.

    FAQs

    What are the ETAuto EV Awards 2026?

    The ETAuto EV Awards 2026 recognize organizations, startups, innovators, and industry leaders driving India's electric mobility revolution. The awards celebrate excellence in EV manufacturing, battery innovation, charging infrastructure, mobility solutions, and sustainable transportation technologies.

    Who can participate in the ET Auto EV Awards?

    The EV Awards India are open to EV manufacturers (2W, 3W, passenger vehicles, buses, and commercial vehicles), battery and energy storage companies, charging infrastructure providers, fleet operators, technology startups, mobility platforms, and EV component suppliers that have made significant contributions to the electric vehicle ecosystem.

    What are the judging criteria for the Electric Mobility Awards?

    Entries are evaluated by an independent jury based on factors such as innovation, technology advancement, business impact, market adoption, scalability, sustainability, and overall contribution to the growth of India's electric mobility sector. A transparent multi-stage evaluation process ensures fairness and credibility.

    How can I nominate my company for the ETAuto EV Awards?

    Participants can submit their nominations through the official ETAuto EV Awards website by creating an account, selecting the relevant award category, completing the nomination form, uploading the required supporting documents, and submitting the entry before the deadline.

    Why should companies enter the ET Auto EV Awards?

    Winning or being shortlisted at the Electric Vehicle Awards helps organizations gain industry recognition, strengthen brand credibility, showcase innovation, attract new business opportunities, generate media visibility, and connect with leading automotive and mobility decision-makers.
