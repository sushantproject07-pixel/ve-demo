Overview

2023: Is travel & tourism ready for new dynamics?

Pre-pandemic, in 2019, the global market size for MICE was in excess of USD 808 billion and the market size of MICE in India was INR 37,576 crores in which nearly 60% came from MIC (Meetings, Incentives and Conferences) segment. Of these, 65% were B2B events. The estimated market size from Events was in the tune of INR 4,800 crores from space rental but it had a multiplier impact on the economy in terms of accommodation and travel.

Cut to the present, at the end of 2024, business travel spending is expected to reach USD 1.4 trillion and make a full recovery from the pandemic in the succeeding year. Predictive research indicates that 1.2 to 1.5 million Indians will travel for MICE purposes all over the world and are likely to spend around USD 1.5 billion in the coming year.

Read More...

In the wedding segment, the USD 50-billion wedding market in India seems to have rebounded from the pandemic woes with 3.2 million events lined up for the November 2022-February 2023, making a sharp spike from 2.5 million a year back. Research indicates that the spending is also likely to reach INR 3.75 lakh crore this season, up from INR 3 lakh crore last year.

ETTravelWorld MICE & Wedding Tourism Summit and Awards is to be the biggest platform for MICE buyers, comprising India’s top corporate houses, tourism and hospitality professionals, destinations, events and wedding planners. The event will present an opportunity for the industry players to collaborate with buyers and planners for business prospects.

While the summit will explore the emerging trends in corporate business travel as well as the increasing grandeur of the great Indian wedding and engage the industry stakeholders in serious and meaningful deliberations, the awards will felicitate the forerunners in the industry.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who is MICE & Wedding Tourism Summit - Corporate Travel Event & Conference for?

The MICE & Wedding Tourism Summit - Corporate Travel Event & Conference is exclusively for Travel Industry Professionals. It caters to event planners, destination management companies, wedding planners, hoteliers, tourism boards, industry suppliers, and other related professionals.

What will ET Corporate Tourism events & Summit have to offer?

The ET Corporate Tourism events & Summit offer a diverse range of offerings, including insightful panel discussions, keynote presentations by industry experts, networking opportunities with renowned professionals, interactive workshops, a showcase of innovative products and services, and an awards ceremony honoring the best performers in the industry. It is a platform to gain knowledge, network, and explore the latest trends and opportunities in the corporate travel, MICE, and wedding tourism sectors.

How can I attend the event? Do I need to buy tickets?

Attendance for the MICE & Wedding Tourism Summit - Corporate Travel Event & Conference is free of cost; however, it is restricted to Travel Industry Professionals. To attend the event, you need to register online through our official website and provide the required information. Upon successful registration, you will receive a confirmation email with your event pass.

Can I purchase my ticket onsite on the day of the event?

As the event is free of cost, there is no need to purchase a ticket onsite. However, we recommend registering in advance through our official website to secure your spot and receive event updates and important information. Walk-ins may be accommodated based on availability, but we strongly advise pre-registration to guarantee your attendance.
