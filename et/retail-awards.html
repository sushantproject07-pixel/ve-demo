Overview

After going through tumultuous seas in the year 2020, India’s Retail Industry is on a robust path to recovery. Consumers are back on streets, brands are launching new products and retail outlets are seeing continuous walking of customers, both online and offline, anticipating a progressive next year. As per reports, India's retail industry is projected to grow at 9% over 2019-2030, from US$ 779 billion in 2019 to US$ 1,407 billion by 2026 and more than US$ 1.8 trillion by 2030. Both traditional and as well as new age players are relentlessly working towards building a robust retail economy in the country on the back of multiple tools and technologies to drive seamless omnichannel experiences for the customers.

ET Retail has been closely working with the industry leaders and has launched the first edition of ET Retail Awards that will celebrate the excellence of key players and stakeholders in the industry. This will be the biggest congregation of retail players under one roof where they will showcase their innovations, growth strategies, marketing, scale, and other aspects of the business. The nominations will be driven in five categories - retail, ecommerce, D2C players, marketing and special categories.

METHODOLOGY

1. Our ETRetail Awards are open to Retailers, e-Retailers, retail chains, malls, e-commerce sites, D2C brands and Individuals

2. Once the nomination is received by us, they will be sorted and presented to our regional and national juries consisting of industry doyens and experts.

3. The Juries will then mark each entry numerically and these will then be tabulated and audited to arrive at a winner in each category and sub-category.

ABOUT ETRetail.com

ETRetail is the retail news vertical of The Economic Times. It offers in-depth news, analysis, views, interviews, podcasts and global trends in the retail sector.
