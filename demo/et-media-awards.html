Overview

Celebrating Creativity,Innovation &StrategicExcellence.

The ET Media & Entertainment Awards celebrate creativity, innovation and strategic excellence that shape today's media consumption landscape.

These awards recognise the smartest, most impactful campaigns, promos, trailers and teasers for brands, products, services or content titles — including theatrical movies, film franchises, TV shows, web originals, music videos, movie videos, branded content and brand-led entertainment properties.

Honouring breakthrough marketing and promotional efforts, the awards spotlight campaigns that elevate audience engagement and drive brand growth.

Why Enter ETBrandEquity Media Awards 2026?

Media & Entertainment Awards celebrates and felicitates the best work across digital, media, marketing, and streaming business.

Strengthen your brand presence and gain industry-wide recognition by showcasing outstanding work on a respected platform.

How Our Awards Work

Judging Criteria

We focus on exceptional entries that demonstrate outstanding execution and strong impact. Judges consider aspects like innovation, inspiration, and the ability to push boundaries.

Fundamental

Persistent Fairness & Integrity

Judges who enter the competition don't vote on entries in their category to avoid bias. A strict code of conduct ensures impartial evaluation of all entries, so only the most exceptional work receives recognition.

Integrity First

Confidentiality

All material remains confidential to the judges. Only the 'Credits and Case Study' section of an entry is made public via the shortlist/winner announcement. If you win, our team may summarise the entry for winner guides.

About ETBrandEquity.com

ETBrandEquity.com is the online avatar of Brand Equity, India's premier source of information, news and opinion on the marketing and advertising industries.

For over two decades as a weekly print magazine and for six years as an incendiary TV show, Brand Equity has set the agenda for the Indian marketing and marketing communications industry, chronicling the latest trends, the larger than life personalities and the hottest campaigns and agencies.

ETBrandEquity will be the single largest, most reliable source for information on the latest in the Indian marketing industry: from market share battles, account movements and pitches, quarterly performances of leading consumer goods companies, and the latest movements of key industry personnel.
